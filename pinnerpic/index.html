How it works

From article photos to a finished pin in three steps — no design software, no templates to install.

Step 1

Paste or drop your photos in

Copy a photo straight from your article (right-click → “Copy image”) and press Ctrl+V anywhere on the page, or drag & drop image files from your computer, or click Upload photos. Photos fill the pin slots in order.

Step 2

Type the title, pick a layout, style it

Write your title line by line, highlight the keyword in the accent colour, choose one of seven layouts, and fine-tune the stroke, font size, line spacing and band colour until it looks right.

Step 3

Download the PNG and post it

One click exports a crisp 1000 × 1500 PNG — the exact 2:3 size Pinterest recommends — with your site name printed at the bottom, ready to upload to Pinterest.

Frequently asked questions

The short answers, before you ask.

Do I need an account?

No — the tool is free and open, no account needed.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser — photos you paste, drop or upload are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server. Close the tab and they are gone.

What size is the downloaded pin?

Every pin downloads as a PNG at exactly 1000 × 1500 pixels — Pinterest’s recommended 2:3 ratio — so it uploads crisp with no cropping surprises.

Can I brand pins with my own site name?

Yes. Type any site name into the branding field and it is printed at the bottom of every pin. The tool works for any blog or niche — food, home decor, hairstyles, fashion, crafts and more — and the name is never fixed or hardcoded.

How many photos does each layout use?

Collage uses four photos (two above and two below the title band). 6-Photo Collage uses six photos (two above and four below the title band in a 2x2 grid). 8-Photo Collage uses eight photos (two above and six below the title band in a 3x2 grid). Text Overlay uses one full-bleed photo. Top Text uses one full-bleed photo below the title band. Bottom Panel uses one photo above the title panel. Side-by-Side uses two photos, next to each other above the title panel. Paste or drop more photos and tap a slot to swap any of them.

Does it cost anything?

No — the generator is free to use, with no sign-up and no limits on how many pins you make.
