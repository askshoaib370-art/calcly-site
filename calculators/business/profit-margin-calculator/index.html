How the Profit Margin Calculator works

Margin % = ((price − cost) / price) × 100. Markup % = ((price − cost) / cost) × 100. Example: a $40 cost sold for $65 gives a 38.5% margin but a 62.5% markup — the same dollar profit expressed two different ways.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between margin and markup?
Margin is profit as a percent of selling price; markup is profit as a percent of cost &mdash; they're always different numbers for the same sale.
What's a good profit margin?
It varies widely by industry &mdash; retail often runs 20-50%, while services and software can run much higher.
How do I price for a target margin?
Price = cost / (1 &minus; target margin), e.g. for a 40% margin on a $40 cost: 40 / 0.6 = $66.67.
