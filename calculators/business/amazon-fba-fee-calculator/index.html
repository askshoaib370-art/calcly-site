How the Amazon FBA Fee Calculator works

Net profit = sale price − product cost − referral fee − fulfillment fee − storage fee. The referral fee is a percentage of sale price (varies by category, commonly 8-17%); fulfillment and storage fees come from Amazon's published FBA rate tables based on size and weight tier.

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the FBA fulfillment fee?
Picking, packing, shipping, and customer service for that order, based on your product's size and weight tier.
How do I lower my FBA fees?
Optimizing product dimensions and weight to stay within a lower size tier is the most common way to reduce fulfillment costs.
Does this include advertising costs?
No, enter any PPC/advertising spend per unit separately if you want it reflected in your true profit.
