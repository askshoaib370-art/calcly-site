How the Break-Even Point Calculator works

Break-even units = fixed costs / (price per unit − variable cost per unit). The denominator is your \"contribution margin\" — what each unit contributes toward covering fixed costs after its own variable cost.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a fixed cost?
Rent, salaries, and insurance &mdash; costs that don't change with how many units you sell.
What if my variable cost is higher than my price?
You can never break even; you lose money on every unit sold, regardless of volume.
Does break-even mean I'm profitable?
No &mdash; it means you've covered costs exactly. Every unit sold beyond break-even is profit.
