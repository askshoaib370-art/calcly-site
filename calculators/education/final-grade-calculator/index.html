How the Final Grade Calculator works

Desired grade = (current grade × (1 − exam weight)) + (final exam score × exam weight). Solving for the final exam score gives: needed score = (desired grade − current grade × (1 − weight)) / weight.

Frequently asked questions

What if the required score is over 100%?
It means that grade isn't achievable from the final exam alone &mdash; check for extra credit or a different weighting.
Does this work for any weighting system?
Yes, enter your exam's weight as a percentage of the total final grade.
What if I don't know my current grade exactly?
Use your best estimate from your syllabus's grade breakdown; small errors won't change the needed score dramatically.
