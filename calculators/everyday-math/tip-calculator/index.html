How the Tip Calculator works

Tip = bill × tip percentage. Total = bill + tip. When splitting, the total is divided evenly by the number of people.

Frequently asked questions

What's a standard tip percentage?
15-20% is standard in the US for good service at a sit-down restaurant.
How do I split a bill unevenly?
This calculator splits evenly; for itemized splitting, each person should total their own items plus a proportional share of tax and tip.
Should tip be calculated before or after tax?
Etiquette varies, but many people calculate tip on the pre-tax subtotal.
