Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my age just years divided evenly?
Because months have different lengths and leap years add an extra day every 4 years, so simple division doesn't give an exact age.
Can I calculate age as of a future or past date?
Yes, enter any reference date instead of leaving it on today.
Does this account for leap years?
Yes, it uses actual calendar dates, so leap years are handled automatically.
