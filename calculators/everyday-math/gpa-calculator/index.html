How the GPA Calculator works

GPA = Σ(grade points × credit hours) ÷ Σ(credit hours). Each letter grade is converted to its standard point value (A=4.0, A-=3.7, etc.), multiplied by that course's credit hours, summed, then divided by total credit hours.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?
Weighted GPA gives extra points for AP, IB, or honors courses (sometimes up to 5.0 scale); unweighted treats every course equally on a 4.0 scale.
How do I calculate cumulative GPA across semesters?
Total all grade points earned across every semester, divided by total credit hours attempted across all semesters.
Does a withdrawal (W) affect GPA?
Typically no, a W is excluded from GPA calculations at most schools, though policies vary.
