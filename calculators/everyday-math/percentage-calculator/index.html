How the Percentage Calculator works

Three common percentage calculations: X% of Y = (X/100)×Y; X as a percent of Y = (X/Y)×100; percent change from X to Y = ((Y−X)/X)×100. Example: 40% of 160 is 64, and the change from 40 to 160 is a 300% increase.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage increase?
Use ((new &minus; old) / old) &times; 100 &mdash; a positive result is an increase, negative is a decrease.
How do I find 15% of a number?
Multiply the number by 0.15, or enter 15 as X and the number as Y above.
Why is X as a percent of Y different from X% of Y?
They answer different questions: one scales Y by a percentage, the other expresses X relative to Y as a percentage.
