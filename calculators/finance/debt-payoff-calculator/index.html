How the Debt Payoff Calculator (Snowball Method) works

The snowball method applies every extra dollar to your smallest balance first, while paying the minimum on everything else. Once the smallest debt is paid off, its entire payment rolls into the next-smallest debt, snowballing until every debt is gone. This calculator simulates that process month by month across up to three debts.

Frequently asked questions

Snowball vs. avalanche method?
Snowball pays off the smallest balance first for quick psychological wins; avalanche pays the highest interest rate first to save more money overall.
Does this include interest?
This simplified version models principal paydown only; real payoff timelines will be longer once interest accrual is factored in.
What if I have more than 3 debts?
List your largest debts here, or group smaller ones together, to get a close estimate.
