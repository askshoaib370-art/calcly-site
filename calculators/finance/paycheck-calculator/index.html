How the Paycheck Calculator works

Your gross pay has federal income tax, FICA (a flat 7.65% for Social Security and Medicare), and state income tax withheld. This calculator applies simplified, approximate 2025-style federal brackets and a state rate you select, then divides the result by your pay frequency. Actual withholding also depends on filing status, dependents, and deductions, so treat this as an estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my take-home pay different from my salary?
Federal tax, state tax, and FICA are withheld from every paycheck, so your net pay is always lower than your gross salary.
What is FICA?
A combined 7.65% tax (6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare) that funds those federal programs.
Is this exact?
No — it's a simplified estimate. Your actual withholding depends on your W-4 elections, filing status, and local taxes.
