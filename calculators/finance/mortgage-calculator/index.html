How the Mortgage Calculator works

This calculator uses the standard amortization formula M = P[r(1+r)n]/[(1+r)n−1], where P is your loan amount (home price minus down payment), r is your monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments. Example: a $280,000 loan at 6.5% for 30 years comes to about $1,769/month, with roughly $356,000 paid in interest over the life of the loan.

Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford?
A common guideline is spending no more than 28% of your gross monthly income on housing costs, including principal, interest, taxes, and insurance.
Does this include property taxes and insurance?
No, this calculator shows principal and interest only. Add your local property tax rate and homeowner's insurance estimate for a full monthly PITI payment.
What's a good down payment?
20% avoids private mortgage insurance (PMI) on conventional loans, but many loan programs allow much less.
