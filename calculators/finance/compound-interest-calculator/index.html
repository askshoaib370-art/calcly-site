How the Compound Interest Calculator works

This uses A = P(1 + r/n)nt, where P is your starting amount, r is the annual rate, n is how many times per year interest compounds, and t is the number of years. Example: $5,000 at 7% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about $10,048, meaning roughly $5,048 was earned purely from interest.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between simple and compound interest?
Compound interest earns interest on previously earned interest, so growth accelerates over time; simple interest only applies to the original principal.
How often should interest compound for the best growth?
Daily or monthly compounding grows slightly faster than annual compounding, though the real-world difference is small at typical rates.
Does this account for additional contributions?
No, this version assumes a single lump sum with no further deposits.
