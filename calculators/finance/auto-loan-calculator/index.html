How the Auto Loan Calculator works

This calculator subtracts your down payment and trade-in value from the vehicle price to get the amount financed, then applies the standard loan amortization formula across your chosen term. Example: financing $29,000 at 7.2% for 60 months comes to about $577/month.

Frequently asked questions

What's a good interest rate for a car loan?
Rates vary widely by credit score and loan age, but good credit typically sees roughly 5-9% on new cars as of recent years.
Should I choose a 60 or 72 month loan?
Longer terms lower your monthly payment but increase total interest paid and raise the risk of owing more than the car is worth.
Does this include tax, title, and fees?
No, enter the out-the-door price if you want those included, or treat this as principal and interest only.
