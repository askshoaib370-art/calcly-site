How the Roofing Calculator works

A roofing \"square\" equals 100 sq ft of roof area. Squares = (roof area × (1 + waste%)) ÷ 100, and most standard shingles come 3 bundles to a square.

Frequently asked questions

What's a roofing 'square'?
100 square feet of roof area &mdash; the standard unit roofers use for estimating materials.
How much extra should I order?
Add about 10% for waste, cuts, and ridge caps; steep or complex roofs may need more.
Does this account for roof pitch?
No, enter the actual sloped surface area, which is larger than the home's footprint on a pitched roof.
