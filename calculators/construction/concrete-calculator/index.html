How the Concrete Calculator works

Cubic yards = (length × width × depth in feet) ÷ 27. Depth is entered in inches and converted to feet. Example: a 20×10 ft slab at 4 inches deep needs about 2.47 cubic yards. Most contractors add 5-10% extra to cover spillage and an uneven subgrade.

Frequently asked questions

How much extra concrete should I order?
Most contractors add 5-10% to account for spillage and an uneven subgrade.
How thick should a driveway slab be?
Typically 4 inches for passenger cars, 6 inches for driveways that will carry heavier vehicles.
How many 80 lb bags make a cubic yard?
Roughly 54 bags of 80 lb bagged concrete mix make one cubic yard.
