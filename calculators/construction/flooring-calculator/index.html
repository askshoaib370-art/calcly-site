How the Flooring Calculator works

Room area = length × width. We add your waste percentage to that area, then divide by the coverage per box (from the product packaging) and round up, since boxes must be bought whole.

Frequently asked questions

Why add 10% extra?
To cover cuts, mistakes, and future repairs made with material from the same batch and dye lot.
Does this work for tile, laminate, and hardwood?
Yes &mdash; just enter the coverage per box or carton listed on your specific product's packaging.
Should I round up or down on boxes?
Always round up; partial boxes still need to be purchased in full.
