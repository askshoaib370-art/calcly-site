How the Mulch Calculator works

Cubic yards = (area in sq ft × depth in inches) ÷ 324. Example: a 200 sq ft bed at 3 inches deep needs about 1.85 cubic yards, or roughly 25 standard 2 cu ft bags.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should mulch be?
2-3 inches is standard &mdash; enough to suppress weeds without smothering plant roots.
How many bags is a cubic yard?
Roughly 13-14 standard 2 cubic foot bags make one cubic yard.
Should I remove old mulch first?
Not usually &mdash; a couple inches of old, decomposed mulch can stay; just top it off to your target depth.
