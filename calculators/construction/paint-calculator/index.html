How the Paint Calculator works

Gallons = (paintable wall area × number of coats) ÷ 350, since one gallon of paint typically covers about 350 sq ft per coat. Doors are estimated at 20 sq ft and windows at 15 sq ft each and subtracted from the gross wall area.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to subtract windows and doors?
Yes, for accuracy subtract their combined area from the total wall area &mdash; this calculator does it automatically using standard estimates.
How many coats should I plan for?
Two coats is standard for full, even color coverage, especially over a different base color.
Does one gallon really cover 350 sq ft?
That's the typical manufacturer estimate for smooth walls; textured surfaces may need more.
