How the Calorie Calculator (TDEE) works

This uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), then multiplies it by an activity factor to get TDEE — the calories you burn in a typical day. A deficit of about 500 calories/day from TDEE tends to produce roughly 1 lb of weight loss per week, and a 500-calorie surplus tends to produce roughly 1 lb of gain per week.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?
A deficit of about 500 calories/day below your TDEE typically produces around 1 lb of weight loss per week.
What is TDEE?
Total Daily Energy Expenditure &mdash; all the calories you burn in a day, including activity, not just resting metabolism.
Is this accurate for everyone?
It's a population-average estimate; individual metabolism can vary by 10% or more in either direction.
