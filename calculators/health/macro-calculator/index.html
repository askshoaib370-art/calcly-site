How the Macro Calculator works

Protein and carbs provide about 4 calories per gram, and fat provides about 9 calories per gram. This calculator splits your daily calorie target into grams using the percentage split for your chosen goal. Example: 2,200 kcal on a balanced 30/40/30 split works out to about 165g protein, 220g carbs, and 73g fat.

Frequently asked questions

What macro split is best for fat loss?
A common starting point is roughly 40% protein / 30% carbs / 30% fat, though any split that fits your calorie target and preferences can work.
Do I need to hit my macros exactly every day?
No &mdash; weekly averages matter far more than hitting an exact number every single day.
How do I find my calorie target?
Use a TDEE/calorie calculator first, then apply a deficit or surplus for your goal before splitting into macros.
