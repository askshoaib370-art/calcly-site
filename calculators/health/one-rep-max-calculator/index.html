How the One-Rep Max Calculator works

This uses the Epley formula: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps/30). Example: lifting 185 for 5 reps estimates a one-rep max of about 216. The percentage breakdowns below are commonly used to plan training weights for strength programs.

Frequently asked questions

Is this accurate for high rep sets?
Accuracy decreases above about 10 reps; the formula is most reliable for sets of 1-5 reps.
How often should I test my actual 1RM?
Most lifters test every 8-12 weeks to track progress while avoiding the injury risk of frequent true max attempts.
Does this work the same for any exercise?
It works reasonably well for most barbell compound lifts; isolation exercises can behave differently.
