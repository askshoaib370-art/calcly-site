How the Pregnancy Due Date Calculator works

This adds 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period, the standard method used by most due-date estimators and healthcare providers (Naegele's rule).

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this estimate?
Only about 5% of babies are born exactly on their due date; think of it as the center of a roughly two-week window.
Can I calculate from a conception date instead?
Yes &mdash; add 266 days to the conception date instead of 280 days from the last period.
Does cycle length affect this?
Yes, this assumes a standard 28-day cycle; shorter or longer cycles shift the actual due date somewhat.
