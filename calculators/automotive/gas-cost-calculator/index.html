How the Gas Cost Calculator works

Gallons needed = distance ÷ MPG. Total cost = gallons × price per gallon. Example: a 300-mile trip at 28 MPG with gas at $3.45/gal costs about $36.96.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my car's actual MPG?
Divide miles driven by gallons used at your last fill-up, or check your owner's manual for the EPA-rated combined MPG.
Does this account for highway vs. city driving?
Enter your combined MPG for the most realistic estimate, or use your highway MPG for a long road trip.
Does this include tolls or other trip costs?
No, this covers fuel cost only.
